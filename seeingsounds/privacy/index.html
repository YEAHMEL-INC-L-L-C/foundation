Seeing Sounds

Privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026. Seeing Sounds is run by YEAHMEL INC LLC ("we", "us").

Seeing Sounds measures the music you give it and turns those measurements into visuals. We built it to keep as little about you as possible. This policy explains what happens to your information in the web app at app.yeahmel.com and in the Seeing Sounds desktop and mobile apps.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Audio you upload to the web app is used only to analyze it, and is deleted before the analysis runs. We do not keep your audio or your results.
  • The desktop and mobile apps analyze your music on your own device. Your audio is not sent to us.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Audio you analyze

In the web app, when you choose a track, your browser sends the audio file to our server on Google Cloud Run in the United States. The server saves it to temporary storage just long enough to read it, deletes that copy, and then calculates the analysis (such as tempo, key and beat grid) and the visualizer data from the audio held in memory. It sends the results back to your browser and keeps nothing once the request is finished. If our server cannot be reached, your browser does the analysis itself and nothing is uploaded.

In the desktop and mobile apps, all analysis happens on your device.

Videos and reports you export

Videos and JSON reports are made on your device and saved or shared only where you choose. They are not uploaded to us.

Your design settings

The title, artist name, font and colours you choose in the Design panel are saved in your browser's or app's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Information our hosting records

Like most websites, our hosting provider, Google Cloud, automatically logs basic details of each request to our server: your IP address, the date and time, the page or address requested, your browser and device type, and the size of the response. These logs do not contain your audio. We use them only to keep the service running and secure, and they are deleted automatically, normally within 30 days. Google processes this data for us under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Cookies

Seeing Sounds does not use cookies. Fonts are served from our own server, so no third party learns that you visited.

Children

Seeing Sounds is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we add a feature that handles your information differently, such as AI-written feedback on a song, we will update this policy and the effective date before that feature uses your audio.
